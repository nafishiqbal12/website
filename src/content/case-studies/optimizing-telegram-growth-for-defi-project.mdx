## Background

NeoFi launched an ambitious liquidity mining program but struggled with community retention and onboarding. Our goal was to scale acquisition while preserving quality.

## Approach

- Built onboarding funnels and gated educational sequences
- Partnered with niche creators to seed trust
- Introduced weekly AMAs and targeted airdrops for retention

## Results

- Telegram grew from 1.2k to 48k active users in 90 days
- Organic impressions: 3.2M across creator channels
- Twitter gained 6.3k targeted followers
- Measured ROI: 6.8x (attributed campaign conversions)

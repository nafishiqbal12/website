Useful automation starts with a bounded workflow, not a vague promise that an agent can handle everything. Teams should identify the repeated task, define the information and tools it may use, and decide where human approval is required.

## A practical starting point

1. Map the current workflow and its exceptions.
2. Separate routine actions from high-impact decisions.
3. Add approval checkpoints and versioned policies.
4. Observe the delivered workflow before expanding its scope.

This approach lets AI-assisted automation reduce repetitive workload while keeping governance visible and operationally useful.

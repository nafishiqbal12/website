Automation projects often struggle when the workflow is too broad, the approval boundary is unclear, or the team cannot tell why an action was taken. A smaller, observable workflow is usually a stronger starting point.

## Common mistakes

- Automating an undefined process
- Treating exceptions as an afterthought
- Skipping prompt and policy versioning
- Removing human checkpoints from high-impact actions

Controlled automation should make routine work easier to manage while preserving accountability for decisions that require context.

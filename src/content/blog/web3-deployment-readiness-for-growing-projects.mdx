Deployment readiness is more than a successful build. A project should know which environment is being changed, how the release is validated, what happens if the rollout needs to stop, and who owns the next operational decision.

## Readiness areas

- Confirm environment and access responsibilities
- Validate CI/CD checks and release criteria
- Document rollback and operational continuity paths
- Define the first observation and stabilization checkpoints

A structured readiness review gives teams a clearer path from implementation to production without treating deployment as the end of the work.

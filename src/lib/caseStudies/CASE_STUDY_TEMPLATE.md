# Crypto Marketing Case Study Template

This document outlines the professional structure for case studies targeting Web3 founders and blockchain projects.

---

## Case Study Structure (Enterprise Format)

### 1. HEADER SECTION
**Length**: 2-3 lines
**Content**: Project name, blockchain, outcome metric

```
DeFi Protocol Growth: From $5M to $50M TVL in 90 Days
Ethereum + Polygon • Lending Protocol • 900% Growth
```

---

### 2. CLIENT PROBLEM SECTION
**Length**: 2-3 paragraphs
**Goal**: Establish credibility + relatability

**Structure**:
- What was the problem? (specific, quantifiable)
- Why did it matter? (business impact)
- What had they tried? (previous failures)

**Example**:
```
The Challenge
LendingDAO launched with strong tech but zero go-to-market experience. 
After 3 months, they hit $5M TVL but growth completely flatlined. 
Their core problem: no clear positioning in a crowded lending market.

They had tried:
• Direct Twitter outreach (low engagement)
• Generic Discord community building (50 members/week)
• Paid ads on DeFi-specific platforms (poor targeting)
• Ambassador programs (no structure or incentives)

Result: They were losing ground to competitors launching monthly.
```

---

### 3. GROWTH STRATEGY SECTION
**Length**: 4-6 paragraphs with subsections
**Goal**: Show strategic thinking, not just tactics

**Structure**:
- Strategic positioning (how you positioned them)
- 3-5 core tactics (specific, actionable)
- Why these tactics? (reasoning)

**Example**:
```
Our Strategy: Liquidity-First Growth

We identified the core problem: LendingDAO was competing on features, 
not on value. In DeFi, the winner is whoever has the most liquidity first.

Our 3-Phase Approach:

Phase 1: Reposition as "The Liquidity Protocol" (Weeks 1-2)
- New messaging: "$10M in incentives for early liquidity providers"
- Target audience: Yield farmers, LP clubs, treasury managers
- Channels: Twitter Spaces, Discord communities, Telegram groups
- Result: 30% increase in qualified inquiries

Phase 2: Strategic Partnerships (Weeks 3-6)
- Partner with 5 DeFi aggregators (Yearn, Curve, etc.)
- Co-market with complementary protocols
- Get listed on aggregator rankings
- Result: 5X traffic spike, 60% from referrals

Phase 3: Community-Driven Adoption (Weeks 7-12)
- Launch incentivized liquidity campaign
- Ambassador program with 50 active members
- Weekly Twitter Spaces with industry leaders
- Result: $45M TVL increase, viral adoption
```

---

### 4. SEO STRATEGY SECTION
**Length**: 3-4 paragraphs
**Goal**: Show long-term organic visibility

**Structure**:
- SEO opportunity identified (research)
- Keywords targeted (high-intent, low-competition)
- Content created (blog, landing pages, guides)
- Results (rankings, organic traffic)

**Example**:
```
SEO Strategy: Organic Authority Building

Audit Finding: LendingDAO had 0 organic traffic. Competitors ranked for 
high-intent keywords like "best DeFi lending protocol" and "highest yield lending."

Keywords Targeted:
• "How to earn yield on crypto" (60K monthly searches, 50 competitors)
• "DeFi lending comparison" (20K searches, 30 competitors)
• "Liquidity provider incentives" (8K searches, 12 competitors)

Content Created:
• Blog: "Ultimate DeFi Lending Comparison" (ranked #3, 2K organic clicks/mo)
• Landing Page: "Liquidity Provider Incentives Guide" (ranked #1, 5K clicks/mo)
• Guide: "How LendingDAO Structures Incentives" (ranked #2, 3K clicks/mo)

Results After 90 Days:
• Organic traffic: 0 → 15K sessions/month
• Conversions from organic: 80/month (5% conversion)
• Cost per acquisition: $0 (vs $50 from paid ads)
```

---

### 5. COMMUNITY GROWTH SECTION
**Length**: 3-4 paragraphs
**Goal**: Show community-building execution

**Structure**:
- Starting community size
- Growth tactics (Discord, Telegram, Twitter, off-chain)
- Community engagement metrics
- End community size + quality

**Example**:
```
Community Growth: From Inactive to Engaged

Starting Point:
• Discord: 50 members (dormant)
• Twitter: 200 followers (low engagement)
• Telegram: 100 members (spam/bots)

Strategy & Execution:

Discord Community Transformation:
- Restructured channels (governance, development, trading, general)
- Added weekly AMA sessions with founders
- Created "Liquidity Provider" role with exclusive perks
- Launched community contests (weekly trivia, trading competitions)
- Incentivized early members with $500/month in rewards

Result: 50 → 5,000 members, 45% weekly active, 2,000 messages/day

Twitter Growth:
- Daily insights (market analysis, protocol updates, founder thoughts)
- Weekly Twitter Spaces (audience participation, debates)
- Retweet engagement (replied to 500+ relevant accounts)
- Influencer mentions (15 crypto influencers mentioned LendingDAO)

Result: 200 → 8,000 followers, 12% engagement rate

Telegram Groups:
- Moderated channels (no spam, scams removed daily)
- Real-time announcements (contracts, governance votes, yields)
- Exclusive deals (early access to new features for Telegram members)

Result: 100 → 3,000 members (organic growth only)

Community Quality Metrics:
• NPS Score: 8.2/10 (vs. industry standard 6.5)
• Retention Rate: 78% (members staying after 30 days)
• Virality: 40% of new members joined through referral
```

### 6. PR CAMPAIGN SECTION
**Length**: 3-4 paragraphs
**Goal**: Show earned media + brand authority

**Structure**:
- PR strategy (target publications, narrative)
- Placements secured (outlets, reach)
- Generated backlinks (SEO benefit)
- Brand mentions (authority signals)

**Example**:
```
PR Campaign: Earned Media & Authority Building

PR Strategy:
Target: Tier-1 crypto publications (CoinDesk, Decrypt, The Block)

Angle: Technical innovation + market growth story

✓ Decrypt: "Q&A: Inside LendingDAO's $100M Liquidity Incentive Campaign"
  - Reach: 300K+ readers
  - Backlink: High-authority (DA 88)
  - Interview piece (30 min exposure)

✓ The Block: "Market Analysis: Why LendingDAO Is Winning the Liquidity War"
  - Reach: 250K+ readers
  - Backlink: High-authority (DA 92)
  - Analysis piece with detailed metrics

✓ Bankless (Newsletter): "This Protocol Just Changed the Lending Market"
  - Reach: 200K+ subscribers
  - Podcast mention (episode #234)

✓ 15 Mid-Tier Publications: Reviews, interviews, market updates
  - Combined reach: 1M+ readers

Results:
• Total media reach: 2.2M+ people reached
• Backlinks acquired: 20+ high-authority
• Brand mentions: 35+ publications
• Ranking impact: Improved domain authority from 35 → 52
• Direct traffic from PR: 5K+ users
• Lead generation from articles: 200+ qualified leads
```

### 8. ROI SECTION
**Length**: 2-3 paragraphs
**Goal**: Show business impact + cost-benefit

**Structure**:
- Total investment (agency fees, paid ads, software)
- Total revenue generated (new TVL × fee, or customer acquisition value)
- ROI calculation
- Customer lifetime value

**Example**:
```
Return on Investment Analysis

Total Investment (90 Days):
- Agency Services: $30,000 (our consulting fees)
- Paid Advertising: $18,000 (Twitter, Google, DeFi platforms)
- Content Creation: $5,000 (writers, designers, video)
- Tools & Software: $2,000 (analytics, CRM, automation)
_______________________________________________
Total Cost: $55,000

Revenue Generated (First Year Projection):
- Protocol Fees on $50M TVL: $150,000 (assuming 0.3% annual fees)
- Funding raised: $20M (Series A, 50% attributed to brand growth)
- Token appreciation: $8M (secondary market value gain)
_______________________________________________
Total Revenue: $178,000

ROI Calculation:
($178,000 - $55,000) / $55,000 = 223% ROI

Cost Per Acquisition:
$55,000 / 2,450 liquidity providers = $22.45 per LP
(Industry average: $150-250 per LP)

Customer Lifetime Value (LTVs):
- Average LP deposit: $20,400
- Annual fee paid to protocol: $61.20
- Average LP retention: 18 months
- LTV: $918 (protocol revenue) + $20.4K (TVL increase) = $21.3K

Payback Period: 1.2 months (initial investment recouped)

Additional Intangible Benefits:
• Brand authority increase (enables future fundraising)
• Media coverage value: ~$200K in equivalent ad spend
• Backlinks acquired: $50K in SEO value
• Founder credibility: Enables strategic partnerships worth $2M+
```

## Case Study Variations

### Variation 1: Growth-Focused
**Best for**: Rapid scaling protocols/projects  
**Focus**: Community growth, viral loops, referral mechanics  
**Time frame**: 60-90 days  

### Variation 2: Authority-Focused
**Best for**: Enterprise/institutional protocols  
**Focus**: PR, media placement, thought leadership  
**Time frame**: 90-180 days  

### Variation 3: SEO-Focused
**Best for**: Long-term organic play  
**Focus**: Content strategy, keyword rankings, organic traffic  
**Time frame**: 6-12 months  

### Variation 4: Fundraising-Focused
**Best for**: Pre-Series A startups  
**Focus**: Brand story, investor relations, media buzz  
**Time frame**: 90-120 days (leading to funding announcement)  

### Variation 5: Launch-Focused
**Best for**: New token launches / IDOs / NFT drops  
**Focus**: Hype building, scarcity, urgency  
**Time frame**: 30-60 days (pre-launch)

## Case Study Format Options

### Format 1: Long-Form (5,000+ words)
- All 9 sections fully detailed
- Multiple subsections per section
- Extensive data + charts
- High-authority positioning

### Format 2: Medium-Form (2,000-3,000 words)
- Core sections (problem, strategy, results, ROI)
- 1-2 key metrics sections
- Key visuals only
- Balanced depth

### Format 3: Short-Form (1,000-1,500 words)
- Problem, strategy, results
- 3-4 hero metrics
- 1-2 key visuals
- Quick read

### Format 4: One-Pager
- 1-page PDF
- Hero metrics + visuals
- 3-4 key bullet points
- CTA at bottom

## SEO Optimization Tips for Case Studies

1. **Title**: Include client niche + metric
   - "DeFi Protocol Growth: $5M to $50M TVL in 90 Days"
   - Keyword: "crypto marketing for DeFi" + "case study"

2. **H2 Headings**: Use variations of target keywords
   - "DeFi Growth Strategy"
   - "DeFi Community Management"
   - "DeFi marketing results"

3. **Meta Description**: Specific + compelling
   - "How LendingDAO grew from $5M to $50M TVL in 90 days using our DeFi marketing playbook. See the strategy, metrics, and ROI."

4. **Internal Links**: Link to related services/pages
   - Link to "DeFi marketing services" page
   - Link to "community growth" blog post
   - Link to "crypto PR" services

5. **Images**: Use descriptive alt text
   - "LendingDAO TVL growth chart: $5M to $50M over 90 days"
   - "DeFi protocol community growth visualization"

6. **Conclusion**: Include CTA + backlinks to homepage
   - "Ready to grow your DeFi protocol? Book a free audit."

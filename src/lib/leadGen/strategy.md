# Lead Generation Strategy for Blockchain Marketing Agency

Target: Crypto founders and Web3 startups (pre-seed to Series A)

---

## 1. CTA STRATEGY

### Primary CTAs (Conversion-Focused)
1. **"Get Your Free Crypto Marketing Audit"** (Highest-Value Entry)
   - Positioning: Risk-free, specific outcome
   - Placement: Hero section, mid-page, exit intent
   - ConversionRate Goal: 8-12%
   - Outcome: Lead form → Booked call (Calendly)

2. **"Schedule a 30-Min Strategy Call"** (Meeting-First)
   - Positioning: Direct meeting booking
   - Placement: After case studies, service section corners
   - Trigger: Scroll depth (40-60%)
   - Outcome: Calendly popup or redirect

3. **"Join 5,000+ Web3 Founders on Our Newsletter"** (Long-Term Nurture)
   - Positioning: Exclusive insights, weekly tips
   - Placement: Sidebar, footer, multiple mid-page sections
   - Goal: 5-8% email opt-in conversion
   - Outcome: Email sequence starts

4. **"Download [Lead Magnet]"** (Gate Behind Form)
   - Positioning: Immediate value, specific problem
   - Placement: Dedicated landing pages per lead magnet
   - Goal: 15-20% conversion (only visitors interested in that specific topic)

5. **"Claim Your Free [Niche] Growth Roadmap"** (Industry-Specific)
   - Positioning: Personalized to service (NFT, DeFi, Exchange, etc.)
   - Placement: Service pages
   - Goal: 10-15% conversion

### CTA Copy Variations (A/B Test)
- **Urgency**: "Get Your Free Audit (Spots Limited)"
- **Social Proof**: "Join 5,000+ Crypto Founders — Get Free Audit"
- **Outcome-Focused**: "See Your Marketing Gaps in 15 Minutes"
- **Risk-Reversal**: "Free Audit, Zero Obligations"

## 4. CALENDLY BOOKING FUNNEL

### Funnel Architecture
```
Lead → Form (capture email) → Calendly Redirect → Booked Call → Email Confirmation
```

### Form Placement & Triggers

#### Page Loads
- **Service pages**: Sticky "Schedule Call" button (bottom-right, low % opacity)
- **Case study pages**: Mid-page form before related cases
- **Blog posts**: Sidebar form after intro

#### Behavior Triggers
1. **Time on page**: Show after 30–45 seconds
2. **Scroll depth**: Show modal after 60% scroll (mid-page interest signal)
3. **Hover exit**: Show before user leaves (exit intent)
4. **Multiple visits**: Return visitor offer (form appears after 2nd visit with upgrade)

### Form Fields (Minimalist)
- Email (required)
- First name (required)
- Project name (required)
- Blockchain/niche (dropdown: NFT, DeFi, Exchange, Gaming, etc.)
- Current stage (radio: Pre-seed, Seed, Series A, Series B+, Bootstrapped)
- Optional: "What's your biggest growth challenge?" (textarea, optional)

### Post-Form Flow
1. **Instant redirect** to Calendly (pre-populated with email, first name)
2. **Calendly page**: Shows 3 time slots (next 7–10 days), 30-min calls
3. **Confirmation email**: "Your call is booked — here's what to prepare"
4. **Reminder email** (1 day before call): "Quick prep guide + Zoom link"

### CRM Sync
- Forms → HubSpot/Pipedrive (auto-sync)
- Tag by stage, niche, source (landing page, blog, newsletter, etc.)
- Sequence trigger: If booked but not completed, automated nurture sequence

## 5. AUDIT REQUEST FUNNEL

### Audit Types (By Service)
1. **Crypto Marketing Audit** (General, 15-question form)
2. **NFT Drop Audit** (NFT-specific, 12 questions)
3. **DeFi Growth Audit** (DeFi-specific, 14 questions)
4. **Community Growth Audit** (Community, 10 questions)
5. **Paid Ads Audit** (Performance, 8 questions)

### Funnel Steps

#### Step 1: Audit Type Selection
- Landing page: "What Type of Audit Do You Need?"
- 5 radio buttons (with icons + short descriptions)
- CTA: "Get Started"

#### Step 2: Project Details (Progressive Form)
- Project name
- Brief description (2-3 sentences)
- Website URL
- Current stage
- Monthly budget

#### Step 3: Audit Questions (Conditional by Type)

**Example: Crypto Marketing Audit**
1. What's your primary marketing channel today? (radio)
2. Current community size? (number)
3. Monthly marketing spend? (radio)
4. Biggest growth blocker? (textarea)
5. Have you hired an agency before? (radio)
6. Timeline to hire? (radio: ASAP, 2-4 weeks, 1-3 months, exploring)
7. Any competitors you admire? (text)
8. Success metric for next 90 days? (textarea)

#### Step 4: Contact & Scheduling
- Email (required)
- Phone (optional)
- Best time to discuss? (Calendly redirect)

#### Step 5: Audit Report
- Post-submission page: "Your audit is being prepared..."
- Email (day 1): Audit report + 1 key finding
- Email (day 3): "Here's a fix for your #1 blocker" (case study reference)
- Email (day 5): "Ready to implement? Let's schedule"

### Success Metrics
- Step 1→2 completion: 80%+
- Step 2→3 completion: 75%+
- Step 3→4 completion: 70%+
- Step 4→5 submission: 85%+
- **Overall funnel conversion**: 45–55% (form start to form submit)

## 6. POPUP IDEAS (Conversion Funnels)

### Popup 1: Welcome Popup (Page Load, 2-3 seconds)
**Trigger**: First-time visitor OR returning visitor (7+ days since last visit)
**Headline**: "Get Your Free Crypto Marketing Audit"
**Subheadline**: "See what's holding you back from 10K+ community members"
**Content**:
- 3-bullet value prop (e.g., "In 15 minutes, you'll learn your biggest growth gap, specific tactics to scale, exact budget needed")
- CTA: "Get Your Audit" (button) | "Not now" (dismiss)
**Fields**: Email, first name, project name
**Animation**: Slide from right (mobile: slide from bottom)

### Popup 2: Engagement Popup (Time-based, 45 seconds)
**Trigger**: After 45 seconds on page
**Headline**: "One Quick Question..."
**Content**: 
- "What's your biggest challenge right now?"
- 3 options as buttons:
  - "Growing our community"
  - "Paid ads performance"
  - "Launching smart"
**Action**: Clicking option → personalized lead magnet recommendation or CTA
**Exit**: Clicking option or X

### Popup 3: Exit Intent Popup (Mouse exit, 85% scroll depth)
**Trigger**: Mouse leaves viewport (exit intent) OR 85% scroll depth
**Headline**: "Wait! Before You Go..."
**Content**: "Join 5K+ crypto founders getting weekly growth tips"
**Offer**: "Free PDF: Token Launch Playbook (valued at $497)"
**Fields**: Email
**Background**: Semi-transparent overlay with slight blur
**CTA**: "Get the Playbook" (primary), "I'm good" (secondary)

### Popup 4: Newsletter Popup (Scroll depth, 60%)
**Trigger**: After 60% scroll on blog posts or long pages
**Headline**: "Get Weekly Web3 Growth Tips"
**Content**: 
- Social proof: "5,000+ crypto founders"
- 3 benefits: "Weekly tactics, real case studies, early access to launches"
**Fields**: Email (single field)
**CTA**: "Subscribe" + 10% email incentive mention
**Design**: Compact, minimal

### Popup 5: Offer Popup (Return visitor, day 2+)
**Trigger**: Return visitor (2+ visits within 7 days) + NOT yet converted
**Headline**: "Exclusive Offer for Serious Founders"
**Content**:
- "First hour of strategy call = free"
- Limited time: "Valid for next 48 hours"
**CTA**: "Book Your Free Hour" (button to Calendly)
**Animation**: Attention-grabbing pulse/glow effect

### Popup 6: Chat/Form Popup (Scroll depth, 70%)
**Trigger**: 70% scroll on service pages
**Content**: "Questions about this service? Ask our team."
- Form or Drift/Intercom chat window
- Fields: Email, question, phone (optional)

### Popup Timing & Frequency
- No more than 2 popups per session
- If dismissed, don't show again in same session
- Return visitors: Show different popup (e.g., if saw #1 last time, show #3 this time)
- Mobile: Reduce popup frequency (65% of desktop), delay by 5 seconds longer

## 7. EXIT INTENT STRATEGY

### Exit Intent Detection
**Trigger Points**:
- Mouse moves to close browser tab/window
- User navigates to external link without CTA
- User idle for 3+ minutes then tries to leave
- User closes tab

### Exit Intent Offers (A/B Test)

#### Offer A: Lead Magnet + Form
**Headline**: "Grab This Before You Leave"
**Content**: "Download: Crypto Marketing Budget Breakdown (Used by 2,000+ startups)"
**Form**: Email + project stage
**CTA**: "Get the Guide"
**Incentive**: Free checklist + email sequence

#### Offer B: Discount/Free Call
**Headline**: "Hold On! Special Offer Inside"
**Content**: "Book a strategy call before you go — we'll cover your biggest growth bottleneck, no sales pitch."
**CTA**: "Schedule (Free, 30 Min)"
**Subtext**: "Next 5 spots this week"

#### Offer C: Social Proof + Newsletter
**Headline**: "Join 5K+ Founders Getting Weekly Growth Tips"
**Content**: 
- "1 case study, 1 tactic, 1 founder story — every Tuesday"
- "Plus: Free NFT Drop Checklist"
**CTA**: "Subscribe"
**Input**: Email only (frictionless)

#### Offer D: Urgency-Driven
**Headline**: "⏰ Audit Spots Filling Up This Week"
**Content**: "We limit free audits to 3 per week. Spots: 1 left"
**CTA**: "Claim Your Spot"
**Subtext**: "Usually $500 value, free this week only"

### Exit Intent Performance Targets
- **Trigger rate**: 8–15% of visitors attempt to exit
- **Offer display rate**: 80–90% of exit attempts (after 1st dismissal, suppress)
- **Conversion rate**: 5–10% of exit-intent popups convert to lead or email
- **Quality**: 30-40% of exit-intent leads become audit requests or calls

## 11. A/B TESTING ROADMAP

### Priority Tests (Month 1)
- [ ] CTA Copy: "Get Audit" vs "Schedule Call" vs "Book Strategy"
- [ ] Form Length: 2-field vs 4-field forms
- [ ] Hero Message: Outcome-focused vs pain-focused vs urgency-focused
- [ ] Popup Timing: 30 sec vs 45 sec vs 60 sec

### Secondary Tests (Month 2)
- [ ] Exit-intent offers: Lead magnet vs free call vs newsletter
- [ ] Email subject lines: Urgency vs curiosity vs social proof
- [ ] CTA button color: Blue vs green vs red (on each page type)
- [ ] Testimonial count: 3 vs 5 vs 10 testimonials

### Advanced Tests (Month 3+)
- [ ] Lead magnet type by segment (NFT vs DeFi vs Exchange)
- [ ] Personalized popups by source (blog vs service page vs exit)
- [ ] Email sequence timing (daily vs every 3 days)
- [ ] Calendly availability (3 days vs 7 days vs 14 days out)

## HARO Response Templates

### Formula: Quick Value + Credibility + Actionable Takeaway

```
--- HARO RESPONSE ---

REPORTER: "How do crypto projects build engaged communities?"

YOUR NAME: [Full Name], [Title], [Company]
CONTACT: [email/phone]

---

RESPONSE:

The most effective tactic we've seen: 
"Community-first positioning, founder-led engagement"

Why this works:
Projects that treat community as product rather than marketing channel 
see 3x better retention. Our analysis of 50+ projects shows those with 
daily founder engagement maintain 65% 30-day retention vs 25% for 
project-led engagement.

Real example:
We applied this with [Project]. Results: 25K→50K Discord members in 60 
days, 70% 30-day engagement, which attracted institutional interest and 
$5M Series A.

How to execute:
1. Commit founder to 3x weekly AMAs minimum (consistency signals real 
   project, not exit scam)
2. Create structured engagement (voting on features, community-led 
   decisions, transparent roadmap sharing)
3. Incentivize active participation ($USDC rewards for thoughtful 
   community posts)
4. Build transparency (internal project updates, metrics shared weekly)

The biggest mistake:
Treating community team as marketing department rather than core product 
team. Companies that put engineering talent toward community tools vs. 
marketing funnels see better outcomes.

[Full name]
[Title]
[Company]
[Phone]
```

## Quora Answer Templates

### The "3-Point Framework" (Highest Engagement)

```
QUESTION: "How do crypto projects build engaged communities?"

---

The most effective framework we've seen is this 3-part system:

### 1. Foundation Phase (Weeks 1-4): Set Up for Engagement
Structure your Discord:
- Welcome channel with clear value prop
- Separate channels for: announcements, general chat, feedback, support
- Role system (verified, community member, contributor, moderator)
- Automated welcome messages

Why it works: Clarity reduces friction. New members know where to go and 
what to do.

Example: [Project] this structure and went from 20% second-visit rate → 
60%

### 2. Engagement Phase (Weeks 5+): Create Reasons to Participate
Daily activities:
- 1 AMA per week (founder or team member)
- Daily highlights from community
- Weekly feature of community contributor
- Contests/raffles (staking rewards, NFT badges, etc.)

Why it works: People show up for content + connection + recognition + 
rewards

Example: [Project] saw daily activity increase 300% after implementing 
this

### 3. Growth Phase (Month 2+): Motivate Referrals
Ambassador program:
- $100/month USDC for referring 10 active members
- Monthly votes for "ambassador of the month"
- Early access to new features for ambassadors
- Featured in monthly newsletter

Why it works: Network effects compound once you have advocates

Example: [Project] grew from 5K to 50K members using ambassador referrals

---

What most teams get wrong:
Treating community building as marketing tactic rather than core product. 
When founders spend time in community, products improve, users stay 
longer.

Should I expand on any of these? Happy to discuss specific tactics.

---

[Your Name]
Founder, [Your Agency]
```

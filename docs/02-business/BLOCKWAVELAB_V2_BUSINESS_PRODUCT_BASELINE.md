## 3. Vision, Mission, and Value Proposition

### Vision — DERIVED INTERPRETATION

Become a practical technical execution partner for Web3 organizations that
need dependable delivery, governed automation, and operational continuity.

### Mission — DERIVED INTERPRETATION

Help Web3 teams establish, automate, operate, and improve production-oriented
technical systems through explicit scope, human governance, and lifecycle
ownership.

### Customer problem — DOCUMENTED BUSINESS FACT

The documented customer problem is fragmented technical delivery: unclear
infrastructure ownership, fragile release workflows, repetitive operational
work, insufficient operational continuity, and growth activity disconnected
from product and delivery readiness.

### Desired outcomes — DOCUMENTED BUSINESS INTENT

Documented desired outcomes include:

- A clearer infrastructure and access baseline
- Repeatable build, test, deployment, and rollback workflows
- Bounded automation with human approval for high-impact actions
- Explicit operational ownership, runbooks, and incident coordination
- Technical readiness and trust-oriented growth workflows
- Documentation sufficient for client operation after handover

These are intended outcomes, not guarantees of uptime, growth, revenue,
security, or business performance.

## 7. Business Model and Client Lifecycle

The documented model separates:

- Limited three-day trial direction, currently deferred
- One-time implementation
- Production deployment
- Paid observation — the historical approved specification states a **60-day
  observation** period. D-018 records the Founder/Business Owner decision on
  2026-10-09 that the current working policy is **30 calendar days after
  deployment**. The historical BLOCKWAVELAB_V2_SPEC.md statements remain
  unchanged and are retained
  for traceability; the 30-day policy does not claim implementation,
  automation, contractual enforceability, acceptance metrics, or runtime
  verification.
- Stabilization and optimization
- Documentation and handover
- Optional separately paid ongoing service

Monthly recurring service is the primary recurring direction; annual service is
future/optional. D-003-A approves separately priced monthly Managed Operations
in principle when explicitly scoped and approved, but does not authorize
recurring-billing implementation. D-003-B permits documented, case-specific
introductory discounts without setting a cap or authority. D-003-C defers
public prices while permitting approved service descriptions and
inquiry/custom-quotation messaging. No specific price, formula, tax, refund,
cancellation, chargeback, accounting, or recurring-billing policy is
established here.

D-004 was approved by the Founder/Business Owner on 2026-10-09 under Option A:
the Founder/Business Owner retains final pricing and commercial approval
authority unless an explicit, scoped, documented delegation is recorded.
Quotation preparation, scoping, estimating, and review may be assigned to
appropriate roles but do not constitute approval. Discounts require documented,
case-specific approval, a documented reason, and supporting evidence. No
numerical discount cap, monetary threshold, or new approval limit is
established. Non-standard commitments, unapproved bundles, price changes, and
material scope changes require documented approval and applicable change
control. Pricing authority does not grant agreement acceptance, payment
settlement, entitlement, delivery activation, production release, or
accounting/tax authority. Named assignees, delegation evidence ownership,
specialist review outcomes, and any specific delegation records remain open.

Intended client lifecycle:

```text
Qualification
→ Service selection
→ Account/organization/project
→ Onboarding and requirements
→ Proposal and approval
→ Payment confirmation
→ Activation
→ Implementation
→ Deployment
→ Paid observation
→ Stabilization
→ Documentation
→ Handover
→ Optional ongoing service
```

This is DOCUMENTED INTENT. End-to-end runtime behavior is **RUNTIME
VERIFICATION REQUIRED** and the duration conflict remains unresolved.

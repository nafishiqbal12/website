## 2. Authoritative Current Position

The [Owner Decision Matrix, D-013](./BLOCKWAVELAB_V2_OWNER_DECISION_MATRIX.md)
records GROW as a documented capability family whose launch detail is unclear.
The [Charter Decision Register, D-013](./BLOCKWAVELAB_V2_CHARTER_DECISION_REGISTER.md)
requires an approved launch boundary, catalog availability, and acceptance
criteria. The [Project Charter](./BLOCKWAVELAB_V2_PROJECT_CHARTER.md) records
D-013 as unresolved.

Current documented boundaries include:

- GROW is one of the four approved pillars: BUILD, AUTOMATE, OPERATE, and
  GROW.
- The current working catalog contains the Founder-approved
  **Technical Readiness and Trust Systems** service mapped to GROW.
- The GROW boundary is documented as technical growth systems,
  authority/trust content, community enablement, delivery-aligned demand
  support, and feedback loops.
- GROW requires product/readiness, content/community, and feedback-loop
  context; launchable scope remains unresolved.
- Current traceability marks Technical Readiness and Trust Systems as
  `DOCUMENTED / PROPOSED / RUNTIME-UNVERIFIED`; service acceptance evidence is
  absent.
- No current geographic, jurisdictional, pricing, guaranteed-growth, or
  generic marketing-agency commitment is established.

## 3. Documented Capability Versus Launch Approval

### Documented current capability and vocabulary

- The GROW pillar and Technical Readiness and Trust Systems service are
  documented in the business, service-catalog, and traceability baselines.
- The service catalog describes the service as scoped/advisory, with recurring
  work only if separately approved.
- Technical readiness, authority/trust content, community enablement, and
  delivery-aligned demand support are documented boundaries, not a complete
  launch authorization.
- Existing platform, organization, project, commercial, entitlement, delivery,
  and audit foundations provide structural context only.

### Proposed, future, or unverified material

- Initial client-selectable GROW scope, launch packaging, acceptance criteria,
  support model, and measurable outcome evidence remain unresolved.
- Any provider-backed automation or AI runtime remains subject to D-015 and
  specialist review.
- Runtime authentication, authorization, tenant isolation, commercial
  workflow, entitlement, delivery, and release evidence is incomplete.
- Future roadmap or architecture language does not establish a sellable
  offering or production readiness.

### Explicit exclusions

This package does not revive or authorize legacy crypto-marketing-agency
packages, token promotion, influencer packages, guaranteed growth, generic
marketing-agency services, invented service names, prices, KPIs, guarantees,
launch dates, geographic commitments, or unapproved provider-backed work.

## 6. Unsupported Assumptions to Resolve

The following assumptions are intentionally not made:

- GROW is not assumed to be a standalone client-selectable service merely
  because the pillar and one working-catalog service are documented.
- Technical Readiness and Trust Systems is not assumed to include every
  content, community, demand, or feedback-loop activity.
- A client outcome is not assumed to be measurable, attributable, or
  guaranteed without an approved evidence plan.
- Existing platform or catalog structure is not assumed to prove delivery,
  publication, support, provider, or runtime capability.
- A GROW engagement is not assumed to require BUILD, AUTOMATE, or OPERATE, nor
  to be independent of them.
- Documentation or a launch record is not assumed to authorize production
  release.
